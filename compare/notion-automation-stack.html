Pattern 1: Notion as Your Live RAG Knowledge Base

Store your pricing rules, tone-of-voice guide, and support FAQs in Notion pages. Whenever a new lead arrives, your Make or n8n workflow fetches that live context so the AI drafts accurate, on-brand replies.
