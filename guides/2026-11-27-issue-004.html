If you upgraded your team to Notion Business this month expecting "unlimited" workspace AI, check your billing page closely.

Effective November 2026, Notion officially moved Business ($20/member/month billed annually, or $24 monthly) and Enterprise workspaces onto a strict two-bucket monthly AI credit allowance: 1,500 Standard credits (when AI is set to Auto) and 500 Select credits (when you pick a specific model or run Custom Agents).

The catch? Unused credits do not roll over, and AI Meeting Notes alone burn roughly 10 Standard credits per 1-hour call. For a 5-person ops team, paying $100/month just to hit mid-month Select-credit walls—especially right after Notion's upstream Fireworks AI vendor disclosure reminded teams to watch where prompts are routed—is a bad trade.

Here is the honest 2026 cost verdict across Notion, Superhuman Docs (formerly Coda), and ClickUp, plus a 15-minute BYO-key automation you can build today to bypass workspace AI caps altogether.

1. The 2026 Workspace AI Verdict (5-Seat Math)

Notion — Best Knowledge Hub, Expensive as Your Only AI Engine

Notion's Plus plan ($10/member/month annual) still gives you the full database and wiki engine, with only a limited AI trial. Stepping up to Business ($20/member/month annual) bundles the Notion Agent, AI Meeting Notes, and Enterprise Search—plus Notion 3.8 lets Business teams link external ChatGPT Plus or Pro accounts to the Notion Agent.

  • The 5-seat cost: $100/month ($1,200/year) on Business vs. $50/month ($600/year) on Plus.
  • Why teams stay: Unmatched relational databases, clean client portals, and the easiest human-in-the-loop review queue for automated drafts.
  • The honest con: Bundling AI per seat forces you to pay $20/month even for teammates who barely run agents, while heavy builders burn through their 500 non-rollover Select credits in a week.

Superhuman Docs (Formerly Coda) — Best for Maker-Light Teams

Coda officially rebranded to Superhuman Docs, and its "Doc Maker" pricing model remains the cleanest antidote to per-viewer seat bloat. Editors and viewers are free.

  • The 5-seat cost: On Pro ($12/Doc Maker/month annual), a 5-person team with 2 Doc Makers and 3 editors pays just $24/month ($288/year)—saving $912/year versus Notion Business.
  • Why teams switch: Native doc-level buttons, formulas, and Packs turn a single document into a lightweight internal app without extra SaaS seats.
  • The honest con: If all 5 teammates create new docs and schemas daily, Maker seats add up fast—and unlocking unlimited automations and private folders requires Business at $33/Doc Maker/month annual ($40 monthly).

ClickUp — Best All-in-One Ops Value Under $10/Seat

ClickUp recently simplified its lineup into Core ($9/user/month annual), Business ($17/user/month), and MAX ($100/user/month). Core includes 1,000 automation actions/month and 1,000 AI credits/seat out of the box.

  • The 5-seat cost: $45/month ($540/year) on Core, or $85/month on Business.
  • Why ops teams pick it: Tight task hierarchies, native ClickUp Brain summaries, and solid sprint/workload tracking in one tab.
  • The honest con: Advanced Workload views and unlimited Dashboard reporting remain gated above Core, and native workspace automations hit a wall quickly on complex external webhooks.

2. The Zero-Fluff Stack Alternative (Save ~$396/Year)

Instead of paying $20–$33/seat for bundled workspace AI credits, keep your workspace lean and route heavy AI processing through an external orchestrator with your own API key:

  • Lean Workspace + Visual AI Ops (5 seats): Notion Plus ($50/mo) + Make.com Core ($10.59–$12/mo annual for 10,000 operations) + raw OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini API usage (~$5/mo) = ~$67/month (~$396/year saved vs. Notion Business, with zero Select-credit ceilings).
  • Heavy Code or Privacy-First Agents: Pair ClickUp Core ($9/seat) or Notion Plus with self-hosted n8n ($0 Community Edition on a ~$6/mo VPS, or $20/mo Cloud Starter annual for 2,500 executions).
  • Calendar Bottleneck Fix: Use Reclaim.ai (free Lite plan, or $10/seat Starter annual) to auto-schedule focus blocks directly from ClickUp or Google Tasks instead of burning AI credits on manual scheduling prompts.

3. Copy-Paste Recipe: The 15-Minute "Credit-Free" Doc & SOP Summarizer

Stop burning 500-credit workspace caps on routine summaries. Build this 5-step flow in Make.com + Notion (or ClickUp) using your own API key (~$0.002 per run):

  1. Trigger (Watch Database Items): Fire whenever a Notion page (or ClickUp task) has its AI Status property flipped to Queue Summary.
  2. Filter (Loop & Waste Guardrail): Abort immediately if page text is under 150 words or Last Edited By == Automation Bot so you never trigger infinite update loops.
  3. Fetch Page Content: Pull the page body blocks and concatenate plain text, stripping empty dividers and image captions.
  4. BYO-Key LLM Module (OpenAI / Anthropic / Gemini in Make.com): Request strict JSON output containing tldr_3_bullets, action_items (owner + due date), and category_tag.
  5. Write Back & Reset Status: Update the Notion Summary property, append the action items, set AI Status to Done, and send a Slack alert only if category_tag == Urgent.

Result: You save 20–30 minutes a day of manual doc cleanup, bypass monthly workspace Select-credit limits completely, and keep full control over which LLM vendor handles your company data.
