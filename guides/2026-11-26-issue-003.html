2. Honest Tradeoffs (Don't Switch Blindly)

Cheapest on paper isn't always best for your team. Here is the catch with each option:

  • Make.com honest con: Operations add up quickly if you poll every 1 minute or feed spam emails straight into AI steps. Always drop noise with a Filter right after the trigger.
  • n8n honest con: Self-hosting saves thousands, but you own upgrades, Docker, and webhook uptime. If your flows run under 10,000 ops/month, paying $10.59/mo for Make is cheaper than two hours of server debugging.
  • Zapier honest con: Unbeatable for niche legacy connectors and zero-learning-curve speed, but the "Zapier Tax" punishes multi-step AI workflows hard.

3. Copy-Paste 5-Step Audit to Cut Your Bill by 70% This Afternoon

Don't migrate your entire workspace at once. Follow this 15-minute playbook:

  1. Export your top 3 credit hogs: Sort your current automation dashboard by highest monthly tasks/operations. Usually 2 workflows cause 80% of your bill.
  2. Plug them into the calculator: Open the 2026 AI Automation Cost Calculator, toggle Multi-step AI Agent loop if you use LLMs, and check your real annual savings.
  3. Add a zero-cost early Filter: Drop no-reply@, receipts, test submissions, and duplicate webhooks before any AI or enrichment step runs.
  4. Migrate only the #1 noisy flow first: Move linear CRM/marketing flows to Make.com (grab the 1-month Core + 10k credits free trial) or move looping AI agents to n8n.
  5. Route external outputs through Notion: Log AI drafts into a Notion Needs Review table instead of auto-sending raw replies—and pair with Reclaim.ai if calendar chaos is your next bottleneck.
