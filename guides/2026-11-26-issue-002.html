If you have automations running on Pipedream Workflows (or its AI agent builder, String), your migration clock just started ticking.

Pipedream is shutting down Workflows on March 31, 2027, with all workflow data permanently deleted by April 30, 2027. Waiting until the final week to rebuild brittle webhooks and API glue is how teams break billing and lead routing overnight.

The good news: the no-code and AI automation landscape in late 2026 is much cleaner than it was two years ago. Here is the honest migration verdict across the three best landing pads, how to avoid overpaying when you move, and one high-ROI workflow you can deploy in 15 minutes today.

1. The Migration Verdict: Where Should Your Workflows Go?

Make.com — Best Overall Replacement for Visual Multi-Branch Ops

If you used Pipedream to connect CRMs, webhooks, Notion, Slack, and LLM steps without maintaining servers, Make.com is the fastest high-leverage upgrade.

  • Why ops teams land here: Make's visual router lets you inspect every JSON bundle, add filters before expensive AI calls, and run multi-branch logic at a fraction of per-task pricing. With expanded MCP toolboxes and native support for the latest Gemini, GPT, and Claude models, it handles structured AI workflows cleanly.
  • Migration tip: Replace any polling triggers with instant webhooks and add an early filter module on step 2 so junk events never burn operations.
  • The honest con: If your Pipedream flows relied heavily on custom npm/Python packages inside code steps, Make's native JavaScript/HTTP modules are more constrained—you'll need external endpoints or n8n for heavy custom code.

Zapier — Best for Speed, Zero Maintenance & 9,000+ App Connectors

Zapier has repositioned hard into an AI orchestration platform—folding standalone agents directly into standard Zaps as unified "AI steps" and rolling out self-healing error recovery in Next Gen Zaps.

  • Why non-technical teams pick it: Unmatched connector breadth (9,000+ apps). If your Pipedream stack was mostly standard SaaS-to-SaaS syncs and you want the lowest setup friction, Zapier gets you live in an afternoon.
  • The honest con: Per-task pricing scales painfully on high-volume multi-step flows. A 6-step workflow running 500 times a day will blow past entry tiers fast.

n8n — Best for Code-Heavy Pipedream Power Users

If the main reason you loved Pipedream was dropping raw JavaScript/Python code right next to webhooks and AI agents, n8n is your closest spiritual successor.

  • Why technical builders migrate here: Full Code nodes, flat execution pricing (or free self-hosting), and strong production observability—including OpenTelemetry tracing and audit logs for AI agent tool calls as n8n moves toward 3.0.
  • The honest con: You own the maintenance if you self-host (especially keeping up with security patches on Docker/npm instances), and non-technical teammates rarely enjoy editing n8n canvases.

2. Quick Migration Matrix

  • Multi-branch business ops, webhooks + AI, best cost-to-speed ratio → Move to Make.com
  • Maximum SaaS app coverage, simple linear flows, zero DevOps → Move to Zapier
  • Heavy custom code steps, complex AI agents, self-hosting control → Move to n8n
  • Centralized client knowledge base & post-meeting AI memory → Pair with Notion

3. Copy-Paste Workflow of the Day: The 15-Minute AI Meeting Notes → Notion & CRM Logger

While you're auditing your automation stack, kill the single most hated manual task on every team: copying meeting takeaways into your workspace and CRM.

Build this in 15 minutes using Make.com + Notion (plus HubSpot/Attio/Salesforce):

  1. Trigger (Webhook): Fire whenever your AI note-taker (Fathom, Granola, or Fireflies) finishes a meeting summary.
  2. Filter (Save Credits): Ignore internal @yourcompany.com calendar events and short <5-minute check-ins before any downstream step runs.
  3. Router (2 Parallel Branches):
  • Branch A — Notion Meeting Hub: Create a structured page in your "Client Meetings" database with Date, Attendees, AI Summary, Action Items (as checkboxes), and the recording URL.
  • Branch B — CRM Activity Note: Search your CRM by attendee email domain, then attach the cleaned summary + next steps directly to the matching Contact and Deal record.
  1. Optional Team Ping: Only post a 3-line recap to Slack (#client-updates) if the AI summary flags a renewal risk, deal blocker, or new scope request.

Result: Zero manual CRM data entry, every client call automatically indexed in Notion, and your team only gets pinged when something actually needs attention.
